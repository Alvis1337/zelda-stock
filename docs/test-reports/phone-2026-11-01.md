## Failures and surprises

### 1. Checkout assist reads prices ×100 at Best Buy and Walmart (T9, T10) — blocks every real auto-add

What I did: in_stock test alerts via `deploy/zs-api.sh POST /api/test-alert` for items sold by the store itself.
Expected: Best Buy cable ($22.99) added to the cart.
What happened: the status bar said "Best Buy: Price shows $2299.00 (over $560). Not adding: likely a reseller." Every price the app read came out exactly 100 times too large:

| Page shows | App status text |
|---|---|
| $22.99 (Best Buy, Insignia cable 3-pack, SKU 6587233, sold by Best Buy) | "Price shows $2299.00 (over $560)" |
| $1,099.99 (Best Buy, LG 65" B6E OLED, SKU 6680627) | "Price shows $109999.00 (over $560)" |
| $548.94 (Walmart 18175002206, sold by E-Tech Galaxy LLC) | "Price shows $54894.00 (over $560)" |
| out of stock (Walmart 21002656445); a $529.00 bundle in the recommendations carousel | "Price shows $52900.00 (over $560)" |

The decimal point is lost: both sites render cents in a separate (superscript) element, and joining the text gives "2299". As a result a real Best Buy or Walmart restock at $499.99 would read as $49,999.00 and be refused. The last row shows a second problem: on an out-of-stock page the app took a price from a **different product** in a carousel instead of saying it's out of stock.

Evidence: T9-3-bestbuy-price-2299-not-adding.png, T9-4-bestbuy-actual-price-22.99.png, T10-1-bestbuy-tv-price-109999-not-adding.png, T10-2-walmart-price-52900-not-adding.png, T10-3-walmart-marketplace-price-54894-not-adding.png, T9-bestbuy-timeline.txt, T10-*-timeline.txt.

### 3. The restock alarm is silent on this phone while the Pixel Watch is connected (T5, T7)

`dumpsys notification` shows `mListenerHints: 2` (HINT_HOST_DISABLE_NOTIFICATION_EFFECTS) set by `com.google.android.apps.wear.companion/...CompanionNotificationListenerService`, active since 12:20:31 today. Android then drops sound and vibration for every non-call notification, the `restock_alarm` channel included (`mSoundNotificationKey=null`, no vibration or audio player started; the record itself has `mSound=alarm_alert`, vibration enabled, INSISTENT). The alarm still goes to the watch (`WearNotifPipeline` forwarded it). An earlier alarm at 11:28, when the hint was off, did vibrate with usage ALARM. Left as is by the owner's choice, since it's the everyday setup. Evidence: T5-notification-effects-suppression.txt, T5-notification.txt, T5-channel.txt, T5-vibrator-excerpt.txt, T5-audio-excerpt.txt.

### 4. App update resets full-screen intent to deny (T1)

Before the update: `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT: allow`. After installing v1.0.28 over v1.0.22: `deny`, and the app showed "Allow alerts to open checkout on the lock screen without a tap. ALLOW". Every update (sideloaded APK) may switch lock-screen checkout off until the user taps ALLOW again. Evidence: T1-1-app-v1.0.28-fsi-allow-prompt.png, T1-dumps.txt. During the update, Play Protect showed "App scan recommended"; the install went through without a scan being sent (setup-4-play-protect.png, setup-5-app-updated.png).

### 5. A second alert for the same retailer doesn't reopen Checkout while the first is in the shade (T9)

The Target retry reused notification id 607352532 and only updated the existing notification (`mCreationTimeMs` unchanged), so no full-screen intent launched and the screen stayed dark. After the app's notifications were cleared, the same call opened Checkout again in 2.0 s. A real restock that comes after an earlier alert for the same store (still in the shade) would go unseen. Evidence: T9-target-retry-timeline.txt, T9-target-retry-notification.txt.

### 6. Tapping the grouped in-stock notification opens the main screen, not Checkout (T10)

With several in-stock notifications grouped ("Zelda Stock · 2"), tapping the group opened the main activity. Tapping a single notification opens Checkout. When the phone is unlocked the full-screen intent only shows as a heads-up, so a tap is the only way in. Evidence: T10-0-heads-up-in-shade.png, T10-1-bestbuy-tv-timeline.txt.
